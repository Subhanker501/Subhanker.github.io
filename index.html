About Me

I am a theoretical physics researcher with a Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali. My research addresses the statistical behavior of systems driven away from equilibrium, combining analytical approaches and numerical simulations.

My work has explored active-particle dynamics, stochastic resetting, and ordering kinetics in disordered systems. I am interested in developing simple models that reveal how persistence, inertia, friction, memory, and geometry influence nonequilibrium phenomena.

Research

Active Particle Dynamics

Inertial run-and-tumble motion and the role of non-Newtonian friction in dynamical and stationary behavior.

Stochastic Resetting

How resetting protocols modify first-passage times, transport, and relaxation in nonequilibrium systems.

Disordered Systems

Domain growth and coarsening in random-field spin models, including the influence of spatial correlations.

Current Interests

Active matter on curved surfaces, geometrical confinement, memory effects, and collective dynamics.
