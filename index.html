01 / About me

Understanding complexity
through physics.

I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali. My doctoral work connects nonequilibrium phenomena in passive many-body systems with the behavior of inertial active particles in nonlinear environments.

My research interests include active matter, run-and-tumble dynamics, stochastic resetting, nonlinear friction, and the evolution of ordering in disordered systems. I am currently seeking postdoctoral opportunities.

02 / Research

Research directions

From microscopic stochastic models to macroscopic patterns and phase ordering.

01

Active particle dynamics

Statistical properties of inertial run-and-tumble particles, including velocity distributions, diffusion, and confinement.

02

Non-Newtonian media & resetting

Active particle motion in shear-thinning and shear-thickening environments, with a focus on nonlinear friction and velocity resetting.

03

Ordering & disordered systems

Nonequilibrium domain-growth kinetics in random-field systems and the effect of spatially correlated disorder on ordering.
